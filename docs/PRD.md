# Product requirements

OpsVerse is a flight simulator for an AI-operated Indian commerce business. A visitor can launch a seeded business, watch operational events, pause or step time, inject three incidents, inspect source evidence, compare no action against a recovery branch, approve a sandbox action, and replay the event trail. Sales and inventory metrics derive from recorded events. The UI must label deterministic investigation honestly and show money in INR.

Acceptance for the first complete slice: the damaged-return incident records a premature sellable-stock observation, preserves authoritative inventory, explains affected entities, compares branches from the same state, approves quarantine, and verifies recovery. Settlement and supplier incidents follow the same evidence, comparison, and approval workflow.
